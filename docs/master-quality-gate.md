# SoleDeals Sol & Dex master-quality acceptance gate

**This is a production gate, not a claim that a render is approved.**

## Non-negotiable
- Preserve the original user-approved Sol and Dex appearance, silhouette, colors and personality across every frame. The current SVG faces are approximations and require an exact reference match before final approval.
- Real physical posing: prop held in hand follows the root character transform; both wrists contact the object; elbows bend; feet have planted contact; no floating props.
- Intentional shot-by-shot acting: anticipation, action, impact/overshoot, hold, response. Distinct acting poses, no unmotivated repetitive bobbing.
- English male character voices are consistent, expressive, intelligible, and accurately timed against lip and facial animation.
- The Wi-Fi double entendre must make sense without forced sales material; show the jealous reveal and a physically readable ending.
- Composition safe margins, no cropped heads/faces/hands, no subtitle overlap with critical action; readable on a phone.
- Punchline must be held for at least one second; no late text that flashes too quickly.
- Video technical QC: full 18 sec, 9:16, H.264 yuv420p AAC, tested playback on iPhone; no glitch or silence due to missing dialogue assets.
- No secret tokens, API keys or source licenses committed.
- **No automatic posting to TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook until a real finished video has been watched and explicitly approved by the owner.**
